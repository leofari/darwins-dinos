# Darwin’s Dinos

Educational STEM video game developed as part of Wesleyan University’s IDEA 350 course.

🎮 Play the demo: https://darwinsdinos.itch.io/darwins-dinos

## Overview
Darwin’s Dinos is an NGSS-aligned educational video game designed for elementary students.
My project proposal was selected from 20 course proposals and developed by a five-person interdisciplinary team.

## My Role
- Project lead and original concept creator
- Planned iterative development timeline and coordinated artists and programmers
- Created dozens of original backround, character, and music assets
- Developed and delivered a final business pitch to a panel of educators and game developers

## Tools / Skills
Unity, project management, stakeholder presentations, art and music creation, interdisciplinary collaboration, user research
